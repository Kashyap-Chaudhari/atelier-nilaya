2. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal information directly from you when you register, browse, commission bespoke pieces, or acquire jewellery from our boutique:

  • Account & Identity Details: Full name, verified email address, and 10-digit Indian mobile number for patron authentication and order verification.
  • Delivery & Invoicing Details: Physical delivery addresses, billing addresses, landmark, city, state, postal PIN code, and contact person phone number for armored courier dispatch.
  • Financial & Transaction Records: Order IDs, amounts paid, transaction timestamp, and gateway reference numbers. Payment credentials (credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking passwords, UPI PINs) are processed directly by our RBI-authorized payment partner, Razorpay, and are never handled or stored on our servers.
  • Mandatory Statutory KYC (PAN / Form 60): In compliance with Rule 114B of the Income Tax Rules, 1962, we collect your Permanent Account Number (PAN) or Form 60 for jewellery purchase transactions exceeding ₹2,00,000 (Rupees Two Lakhs).
  • Bespoke Commissions & Private Folio Notes: Sizing measurements (ring sizes, wrist circumferences, necklace lengths), design preferences, engraving requests, and consultation notes.
  • Customer Communications: Inquiries, letters, and chat records initiated via email, WhatsApp Business, or our concierge appointment booking modal.
  • Technical & Session Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and cookie markers utilized to maintain your shopping bag and Velvet Tray wishlist.

3. Why We Collect and Use Your Data

Every piece of data collected is processed for specific, explicitly declared, and legitimate business purposes:

  • Account Operation & Patron Verification: To authenticate your client folio, safeguard your account against unauthorized access, and verify ownership of customized requests.
  • Order Fulfillment & White-Glove Transit: To cast, hallmark, laser-inscribe, package, and dispatch your acquisitions via insured armored transport services.
  • Statutory Invoicing & Tax Compliance: To issue legal Goods and Services Tax (GST) tax invoices, maintain hallmarking logs under BIS standards, and fulfill audit obligations under the Income Tax Act.
  • Transactional Notifications: To convey essential order receipts, fabrication milestones, dispatch dispatch manifests, and OTP verification codes for secure delivery handovers.
  • Bespoke Consultations: To schedule private appointments with our master jewelers and prepare tailored design renderings.
  • Curated Marketing (Opt-In Only): To share preview invitations for new haute joaillerie collections solely when you have given explicit prior consent. You may withdraw marketing consent at any time.

4. Lawful Consent and Right of Withdrawal

Under the DPDP Act 2023, personal data is processed based on your freely given, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous consent, or for legitimate statutory uses (such as issuing GST invoices and complying with BIS hallmarking regulations).

How to withdraw consent: You possess the absolute right to withdraw your consent at any time by writing to our Grievance Officer at [grievance@ateliernilaya.com].

Please note: Withdrawing consent does not affect the legality of any processing executed prior to the withdrawal, nor does it affect our mandatory legal duties to retain transactional records and tax invoices under Indian tax legislation.

7. Data Retention Periods

Personal data is retained only for as long as required to fulfill the stated purposes or to comply with statutory mandates:

  • Tax & Invoice Records: Retained for a minimum of 8 (eight) consecutive financial years following the transaction year, as mandated under Section 44AA of the Income Tax Act, 1961 and Section 36 of the CGST Act, 2017.
  • Hallmarking & Provenance Certificates: Retained indefinitely in our internal vault registry to honor lifetime exchange and buyback guarantees.
  • Customer Account Profiles: Retained until you formally request account closure and folio deletion.
  • Marketing Preferences: Retained until consent is revoked via unsubscribe link or concierge notification.

8. Your Statutory Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and the SPDI Rules 2011, you enjoy the following enforceable rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a plain-language summary of your personal data processed by the Atelier, along with the identities of all third parties with whom it has been shared.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You may request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete data, or the erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the stated purpose or statutory compliance.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You have the right to readily accessible grievance redressal mechanisms with our designated Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate: You may nominate another individual to exercise your data principal rights in the event of death or incapacity.

We commit to addressing all verified rights requests free of charge within [30 calendar days]. If dissatisfied with our resolution, you hold the legal right to file a complaint before the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Protection of Children's Data

Atelier Nilaya does not market to or knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age. Precious jewellery acquisitions and account registrations require contractual capacity under the Indian Contract Act, 1872. If we discover that personal data of a minor has been collected without verifiable parental or guardian consent, we will promptly delete the data from our live systems.

10. Security Safeguards & Breach Protocol

We maintain comprehensive technical, organizational, and physical security measures:

  • Encryption: All web communication is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols with HSTS enforcement.
  • Credential Security: Customer passwords are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and salted.
  • Access Control: Administrative access is restricted via multi-factor authentication, least-privilege principles, and IP whitelisting.
  • Incident Protocol: In the event of a confirmed personal data breach impacting your rights, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected data principals in strict adherence to the timelines established under the DPDP Rules and CERT-In cyber incident directives.
