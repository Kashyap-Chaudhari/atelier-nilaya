Heritage & Origins

A Family Bench Begun in 1954

Atelier Nilaya began with a single wooden karigar workbench near Mugalisara in Surat, Gujarat. At a time when families trusted a goldsmith's word above paper receipts, our founder set a simple rule: weigh every gram honestly, purify every alloy thoroughly, and never rush a hand-set stone.

As bridal traditions evolved from ancestral Gujarati families to patrons across Ahmedabad, Mumbai, and beyond, the workshop grew while keeping its core unchanged. Every necklace clasp, temple kadas hinge, and solitaire claw prong is still shaped and finished under the direct eye of our master craftsmen.

Today, whether crafting a heavy bridal trousseau, South Indian nakashi temple jewellery, or a contemporary GIA-certified diamond ring, we honour the same standard forged in 1954 — timeless beauty backed by verifiable certification.
