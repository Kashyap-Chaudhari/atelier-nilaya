1. Our Promise of Provenance

Atelier Nilaya crafts heirloom haute joaillerie. Every jewel that departs our benches in Surat is an original creation formed from certified bullion, responsibly gathered gemstones, and generational bench artistry. These Terms of Provenance constitute the binding agreement between you ("Patron", "Purchaser") and Atelier Nilaya ("the Atelier") regarding every acquisition, commission, and delivery.

2. Precious Metal Purity & Mandatory BIS Hallmarking

In strict compliance with the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 and the Hallmarking Order regulations issued by the Government of India, every piece of gold jewellery crafted and sold by the Atelier bears mandatory BIS Hallmarking:

  • BIS Standard Mark: The triangular Bureau of Indian Standards symbol.
  • Purity Grade:
    • 22K Gold (916): 91.6% pure gold alloyed for structural durability.
    • 18K Gold (750): 75.0% pure gold crafted for diamond and gem settings.
    • 950 Platinum: 95.0% pure platinum tested to international standards.
    • 925 Sterling Silver: 92.5% pure silver with fine anti-tarnish rhodium plating.
  • 6-Digit Alphanumeric HUID: Each individual ornament is laser-inscribed with a unique Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID) code, verifiable via the official BIS Care mobile application.
  • Weight Separation on Invoices: Our legal tax invoices clearly distinguish Gross Weight, Net Precious Metal Weight, and Stone Weight. We never charge precious metal rates for gemstone weight.

3. Gemstones, Diamonds & Laboratory Certification

The Atelier upholds absolute transparency regarding the geological nature of all minerals:

  • Independent Diamond Certification: Solitaires and significant diamond arrangements are accompanied by physical certificate cards issued by globally recognized gemological laboratories [e.g. Gemological Institute of America (GIA), International Gemological Institute (IGI), or Solitaire Gemological Laboratories (SGL)].
  • Natural vs. Lab-Grown Disclosure: Natural mined diamonds and laboratory-grown diamonds are categorized in distinct collections and explicitly labeled in all product descriptions, invoices, and certificates.
  • Heritage Uncut Polki & Kundan: Polki refers to natural uncut diamond slices set in pure 24K gold foil (kundan jadau). Natural inclusions, variations in translucency, and foil reflections are inherent heritage characteristics and celebrate authentic Indian craftsmanship.

4. Ethical Sourcing & Surat Artisanal Standards

Atelier Nilaya is rooted in the artisanal heritage of Surat, Gujarat. We source precious bullion through government-recognized banks and nominate suppliers compliant with the Kimberley Process Certification Scheme (KPCS). Our master karigars work under fair compensation, regulated hours, ergonomic benches, and dignified atelier conditions.

  • Official Atelier Nilaya Certificate of Authenticity & Valuation Card.
  • Independent Laboratory Diamond Certificate (for qualifying diamond pieces).
  • BIS Hallmarking documentation with your specific 6-digit HUID inscribed.
  • Serialized tamper-evident security seal attached to the jewel.
  • Comprehensive Atelier Care & Cleaning brochure.

6. Transparent Pricing Formula

We believe luxury jewelry should be priced with total integrity. Our prices are computed using the following formula:

Final Price = (Net Metal Weight × Daily Bullion Benchmark) + Gemstone Value + Making Charge + 3% GST
  • Daily Bullion Benchmark: Bullion rates are updated daily based on prevailing market rates in Gandhinagar / Ahmedabad / Mumbai.
  • Rate Lock at Checkout: Once your order is submitted and payment is initiated, your metal benchmark rate is locked and immune to intra-day market fluctuations.
  • Goods and Services Tax (GST): A statutory 3% GST is applied uniformly across precious metals and handcrafted jewellery in accordance with Central and State GST legislation.

7. Ordering, Authentication & KYC Verification

Orders placed through our website constitute a formal offer to purchase. An order is confirmed solely upon successful receipt of payment through our payment gateway (Razorpay) or direct RTGS/NEFT bank transfer.

Mandatory KYC for High-Value Acquisitions (Rule 114B): Under Rule 114B of the Income Tax Rules, 1962, quoting a valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) or submitting Form 60 is mandatory for all transactions exceeding ₹2,00,000 (Rupees Two Lakhs) per transaction. The Atelier will request and verify your PAN prior to dispatching qualifying orders.

Right of Refusal: The Atelier reserves the right to decline or cancel orders displaying signs of fraudulent payment, unauthorized third-party cards, or unverified recipient identities.

10. 15-Day Return & Exchange Policy

We want you to treasure your jewel completely. Standard catalog pieces may be returned or exchanged within 15 calendar days from the recorded delivery date, subject to the following conditions:

  • The piece must remain in unworn, brand-new condition with no signs of wear, sizing, or alteration.
  • The serialized tamper-evident security tag must be completely intact and unbroken.
  • All original packaging, velvet box, laboratory certificates, and tax invoices must be returned.
  • Inspection & Refund: Upon receipt at our Surat atelier, the piece undergoes gemological and hallmarking verification within 3 to 5 business days. Approved refunds are credited to the original payment source within [7 business days].

Every authentic Atelier Nilaya creation carries our permanent commitment of value:

  • Precious Metal Value: Credited at [100% of the prevailing bullion market rate] on the day of physical receipt and purity assay.
